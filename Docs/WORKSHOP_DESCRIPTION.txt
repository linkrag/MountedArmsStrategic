[h1]Mounted Arms: Strategic Missiles[/h1]
[b]Your colony's reach no longer ends at the map edge.[/b] Build real-world missile launchers and artillery, pick a settlement on the world map and strike it from home, while a Patriot battery keeps the enemy's answer out of your sky. Powered by Combat Extended's world shelling.

[h2]Launchers[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]M270 launcher (ATACMS)[/b]: crewed tactical ballistic missiles, local map or up to 120 world tiles.
[*][b]M142 HIMARS[/b]: salvos of six M31 GMLRS guided rockets, up to 40 world tiles.
[*][b]M777 howitzer[/b]: fires Combat Extended 155 mm shells up to 60 world tiles.
[*][b]Mk 41 VLS (Tomahawk)[/b]: slow, very precise cruise missiles, up to 400 world tiles.
[*][b]Minuteman III silo[/b]: fires on command only, at settlements up to 500 world tiles away.
[*][b]MIM-104 Patriot[/b]: PAC-3 MSE interceptors shoot down incoming shells, missiles and drop pods (needs CE's "Enable CIWS" option).
[/list]

[h2]Warheads and blasts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Large explosions use real overpressure zones: a vaporized core with a crater, damage rings outward, and mountain bases act as real shelters.
[*]Cluster, thermobaric and EMP warheads for ATACMS and Minuteman. EMP is the answer to mechanoid hives.
[*][b]With Dubs Rimatomics:[/b] fission, W62 and W78 thermonuclear Minutemen with thermal pulse, Rimatomics' mushroom cloud and a 3-day fallout where shelter matters. Plus the [b]RS-28 Sarmat[/b] from its own 4x4 silo: ten warheads spread across the whole map, each one a separate target for your missile defense.
[/list]

[h2]They shoot back[/h2]
Spacer, ultra and archotech factions can retaliate with guided rockets and ballistic missiles. Mod settings let you scale the retaliation budget (0-500%) and, with Rimatomics, opt into nuclear retaliation.
